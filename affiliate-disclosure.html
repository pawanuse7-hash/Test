Affiliate Disclosure

Plain-language information about how this website earns money.

Last updated: 3 November 2026

In short: when you click a product link on DealFinds India and buy on Meesho, we may earn a commission. It costs you nothing extra and does not change the price you pay.

How affiliate links work

DealFinds India takes part in the Meesho affiliate programme. Product buttons such as "View Deal" and "Buy Now" use special tracking links. If you buy after clicking one, Meesho may pay us a small commission. This is how we fund the website.

Does it change what you pay?

No. The price on Meesho is the same whether or not you arrive through our links. Commissions are paid by the retailer, not by you.

Do commissions affect what we show?

We choose products because we think they are useful to our readers. We don't invent reviews, ratings or discounts. A discount label is shown only when we have personally checked that the original price and the offer are real.

Prices and availability

Prices, offers and stock are controlled by Meesho and can change at any time without notice. The price shown on our site is for guidance only. Please always check the final price on Meesho before you buy.

Sample products

While this website is being set up, some products are marked "Sample". Their pictures, prices and links are placeholders used to show how the site works. They are not real offers, and their links do not earn us a commission. They will be replaced with real, verified products.

Our relationship with Meesho

We are an independent affiliate. We are not owned by, operated by or officially connected with Meesho. Meesho and related names and marks belong to their respective owners. We are not responsible for Meesho's products, orders, delivery, refunds or customer service, so please contact Meesho for those.

How links are marked

Affiliate links open in a new tab and are tagged with rel="sponsored" so that search engines understand they are paid or affiliate links. You can also see the notice at the top and bottom of every page.

Prefer not to use our links?

That's completely fine. You can visit Meesho directly and search for the product yourself.
