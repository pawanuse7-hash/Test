About DealFinds India

An independent fashion discovery site for Indian shoppers who like a good find and a fair price.

Our story

Finding fashion you love among millions of listings is hard work. DealFinds India was built to make it easier: we sort styles into clear categories and collections, so you can spot something you like quickly, then head to Meesho to check the latest price and place your order.

What we do

We showcase fashion across women's and men's wear, ethnic and western styles, kurtis, sarees, shirts, T-shirts, jeans and accessories. Each product on our site links to its listing on Meesho through an affiliate link.

What we don't do

We don't sell products

All orders, payments, delivery and returns happen on Meesho, not here.

We don't take payments

There is no checkout and no card or UPI form on this website.

We don't ask you to log in

No accounts, no passwords. Just browse.

We don't copy Meesho

Our design, name and brand are our own, and we don't scrape Meesho's website.

How we make money

When you click a link and buy on Meesho, we may earn a small commission. It costs you nothing extra and doesn't change the price you pay. Please read our Affiliate Disclosure for the details.

Our promises

  • No fake reviews or ratings. We don't invent customer feedback.
  • No fake discounts. A discount tag only appears after we've verified the original price and the offer.
  • Clearly labelled samples. Any placeholder product is marked "Sample".
  • Plain language. You'll always know when a link leads to another website.
