Privacy Policy

How DealFinds India handles information when you use this website.

Last updated: 3 November 2026

1. Overview

This Privacy Policy explains what information DealFinds India ("we", "us", "our") collects, how we use it and the choices you have. We keep things simple: this website has no user accounts, no checkout and no payment forms.

2. Information we collect

Information you give us

If you use the Contact form or subscribe to the newsletter, we receive the details you provide, such as your name, email address and message. We use them only to reply to you or to send the newsletter you asked for.

Information collected automatically

Like most websites, the web host that serves this site may keep standard technical logs (for example IP address, browser type, pages requested and time of visit) for security and to keep the site running. We do not use these logs to identify individual visitors.

3. Cookies and analytics

At the time of writing, this website does not set advertising or tracking cookies and does not run analytics scripts. Fonts and images are served from this website itself. If we add analytics or any similar tool in future, we will update this page and, where required, ask for your consent.

4. Links to Meesho and other websites

Our product buttons open Meesho (or other websites) in a new tab. These links are affiliate links, which means the destination site may place cookies or use similar technology to record that you arrived from us, so that we can be credited with a commission. Once you leave our site, the privacy practices of that site apply, and we encourage you to read them. See also our Affiliate Disclosure.

5. Newsletter and contact messages

If the forms on this site are set up to use an email service or a third-party form provider, your details are processed by that provider on our behalf so that we can receive your message or send you emails. You can ask to be removed from the newsletter at any time by contacting us.

6. How long we keep information

We keep messages and newsletter details only for as long as needed for the purpose you gave them to us, or as required by law, and then delete them.

7. Your rights

Under applicable Indian law, including the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you may have the right to ask what personal data we hold about you, to have it corrected or erased, and to withdraw consent. To make a request, email us at your-email@example.com with the topic "Privacy request".

8. Children

This website is meant for a general audience and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 18. If you believe a child has sent us information, please contact us and we will delete it.

9. Security

We take reasonable steps to protect the information we receive, but no method of transmission or storage on the internet is completely secure.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date above shows when it last changed.

11. Contact

Questions about this policy? Write to your-email@example.com or use our Contact page.
